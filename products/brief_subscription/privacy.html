Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. What we collect

When you subscribe, our payment processor collects the minimum necessary to complete the transaction (payment details, email, and billing address). We receive and store the email address and subscription status needed to grant you access to the brief.

2. How we use it

We use your email to deliver the brief and communicate service updates. We do not sell your personal data.

3. Payment processing

Payments are processed by our merchant-of-record payment provider. We do not store your full card details on our servers.

4. Cookies & analytics

Our site uses minimal, non-invasive technologies. We do not use third-party advertising trackers.

5. Data retention & deletion

We retain subscriber data only as long as needed to provide the service. You may request deletion of your data at any time.
