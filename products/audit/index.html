TirraMind  Portfolio Audit

Your book, as arithmetic.

Paste what you hold. In about ten seconds you get three things your broker app does not show: how much of the movement each line item accounted for against how much of the money it held, which line items moved as one, and what the book did over the same window as its index.

No advice — arithmetic only Holdings are never stored Every number carries its window and its n

One line each: symbol then quantity. Indian symbols need no suffix — RELIANCE 40 resolves to RELIANCE.NS. A pasted broker CSV works too. Share counts or percentages, not both in one paste — mixing them is genuinely ambiguous and gets rejected rather than guessed at.

Or see the output on a book that is not yours
