# Scan: fields outside finance — synthesis (2026-09-07)

Scope: third scan, ten fields outside finance/AI-infra/devtools/crypto, 10 finalists after merge, 17 dropped at merge, ~70 sub-shapes swept empty. Each finalist was attacked by two skeptic lenses (buyer-comparable; moat-incumbents-legal). Note on evidence quality: the WebSearch budget was exhausted before most skeptic passes ran, so verification rests on direct fetches of vendor pages, company registers (Companies House, northdata), GitHub/PyPI APIs and arXiv. Several vendor sites returned 403/404; those gaps are flagged inline.

---

## 1. Verdict

No. Nothing outside finance passed all seven filters with a verified comparable. Ten finalists were produced; six were refuted outright, three survived one lens but lost the other, and one (the PCB signal-integrity toolkit) survived both lenses — but it survived only because Filter 7 permits "NONE FOUND". Not one of the 27 named candidates (10 finalists + 17 dropped) has a comparable that meets the literal bar: <=5 people, no sales team, and a **sourced** revenue figure in the $500k-$1M band. Every "comparable" offered was either (a) a 1-3 person shop with 20-30 years of longevity and undisclosed revenue (Coelho/TOPAS, Crystal Impact, FTG/FilmStar, BioKin/DynaFit, SNIP, AtaiTec, BayesMap), (b) a firm that turned out to be 8-25 people with sales or distributors on verification (Efficient Software 8 staff per Companies House; RoboDK with sales roles and 90 distributors; OptumCE ~21 staff; Sonnet 13; Terrasolid ~15), or (c) a price-point proof only (Micantis $500/mo, camcalib EUR 30-130/mo, AFFINImeter EUR 300-500/yr).

The blunter finding is structural: in every engineering-simulation niche examined, the small vendors that survive for decades are quote-sellers with distributor networks (OptiLayer, OTF Studio, Essential Macleod, Simberian, Geo++, NestFab, BayesMap, Terrasolid). Six independent coating-software vendors over 30 years converging on "contact your distributor" is not a coincidence; it is the buyer's purchasing behaviour. The card-checkout tier that does exist in these niches sits at EUR 25-150/month and is marketed to individuals and hobbyists. The scan therefore did not find a $500k-$1M no-selling solo business in any engineering vertical; it found evidence that such businesses are rare-to-nonexistent in the shapes the owner would build.

Second structural finding: in 6 of 10 finalists the proposed wedge had **already shipped**, free or inside the incumbent, before the scan ran — Slide2 random fields since 2017, TOPAS-Academic V8 "thousands of data sets", PyBOP MCMC + ionworksdata parsers, MUKCa's MIT fixture code with an ABB co-author, sparrow's best-known ESICUP results in a free browser app, DJI Terra's free L2 alignment plus BayesMap's uncertainty maps. The scouts did not check GitHub/arXiv for the exact wedge before proposing it.

## 2. Ranked survivors

### 2.1 CLEAN (both lenses not refuted) — PCB interconnect field solver + Touchstone certifier

- **What:** CLI + web 2D/2.5D BEM field solver for PCB stackups (single/differential impedance, crosstalk, frequency-dependent loss with roughness, RLGC/Touchstone export) bundled with a Touchstone hygiene engine (passivity/causality/reciprocity certification, DC-preserving vector fitting, SPICE/IBIS-AMI export). Field: EDA / signal integrity.
- **Buyer and price:** Hardware/SI engineers at 2-50 person firms doing DDR/PCIe/USB4/MIPI layouts, small fabs/CAM shops quoting controlled impedance, connector/cable/fixture makers shipping S-parameter models. Proposed $99-299/seat/month. **Wound:** no verified incumbent in this niche sells monthly; all verified pricing is annual/perpetual at $495-$36k (Sonnet cart: Lite Plus $495, Level 2 Basic $3,995 — https://www.sonnetsoftware.com/products/sonnet-suites/pricing.html; AtaiTec WooCommerce shop ISD/MPX $760-$17,995 — https://us.ataitec.com/shop/). Engineers do buy SI tools online at four figures; the monthly model is untested.
- **Why big companies avoid it:** Siemens/Cadence/Ansys/Keysight bundle SI inside $20k-100k suites sold by AEs; a $200/mo seat cannibalises the suite and reseller channel. Polar's channel is fabs and CITS test gear. **Corrected:** Altium already integrates Simbeor technology (from AD19) on top of its own built-in 2D solver, so the addressable segment is KiCad/EasyEDA/Eagle users and fabs, not "everyone below $30k". KiCad 9's PCB Calculator is closed-form only (no differential field solve, roughness or crosstalk) — https://docs.kicad.org/9.0/en/pcb_calculator/pcb_calculator.html — so the KiCad-side gap is confirmed.
- **Verified comparable (Filter 7):** **NONE FOUND** with sourced revenue. Nearest shape: AtaiTec (solo founder Dr. Ching-Chao Huang, WooCommerce shop with listed prices, revenue unknown — https://ataitec.com/company/founder/). Simberian (https://www.simberian.com/Company.php, /OrderForm.php) is *not* a no-sales comparable: evaluation-licence request, "contact sales@simberian.com for a formal quotation", login-gated order form, actively recruiting distributors; the RocketReach ~$1M / ~7 people figures are algorithmic and returned 403. Sonnet has 11-50 employees (13 on LinkedIn) and fails the <=5 bar. The claimed "$250/yr Simbeor Verify" anchor could not be verified (pages 404).
- **Moat:** BEM solver with proven convergence, thick-copper/trapezoidal etch, asymmetric stripline, roughness and causal dielectric models validated to 50 GHz against published coupons; batched SIMD/GPU sweeps; robust vector fitting with guaranteed passivity that preserves DC, Kramers-Kronig causality checks with error bounds, reproducible "model quality certificate". **Corrected:** the moat must be won against free tools, not Saturn alone — scikit-rf already ships `vector_fit`/`auto_fit`, `passivity_test`, `passivity_enforce`, `is_passive`, `write_spice_subcircuit_s` (930 stars, v2.1.0 Aug 2026 — https://github.com/scikit-rf/scikit-rf); MMTL (Mayo, GPL) is a free 2D/2.5D coupled lossy-line solver (https://mmtl.sourceforge.net/); FastFieldSolvers (https://www.fastfieldsolvers.com/) does C/R/L; Antmicro gerber2ems (Apache-2.0, 259 stars) does full-board S-params via openEMS (https://github.com/antmicro/gerber2ems). The engine must visibly beat these on a published validation set to justify any price.
- **Kill risk:** The fab does it for free (Polar licence at the fab; JLCPCB free impedance calculator — https://jlcpcb.com/pcb-impedance-calculator; Saturn freeware — https://www.saturnpcb.com/saturn-pcb-toolkit/), so willingness to pay is confined to the ~10% doing loss/crosstalk/via/macromodel work; the buyers who care most already own Keysight/Ansys. Simberian pairing 73 app notes with founder-led quoting after 20 years is the strongest circumstantial evidence that pure card checkout caps well below $1M here.
- **India-legal:** General-purpose EM/SI software is not SCOMET-listed; zero-rated export with LUT; no operator licence; Paddle/Stripe/Lemon Squeezy MoR. SCOMET PDF contents not retrievable this session — claim unverified but unchallenged.
- **30-day public probe:** Ship `pcbfield` on PyPI (2D BEM microstrip/stripline/coplanar/differential with frequency-dependent loss) plus a free web calculator and a free "Touchstone health check" upload page. Publish one correlation post reproducing Polar Si9000 app-note cases, Simberian's public validation cases and the Bogatin Altium-2D-vs-Saturn comparison (https://resources.altium.com/p/hidden-gem-altium-built-2d-field-solver-eric-bogatin-altiumlive-2022), plus a public connector model that makes SPICE diverge. Gate 4-port export, roughness, via models and certified fits behind $99/mo card checkout. **Added gate from the skeptic:** the post must show the tool beating scikit-rf and MMTL on the same validation set. Kill unless in 30 days: >=1,000 installs, >=200 Touchstone uploads, >=25 company-domain signups, >=5 paid checkouts.
- **Fit:** 5. Requires no hardware to validate (unlike the FDTD antenna solver dropped at merge).

### 2.2 WOUNDED — CoatFit: GPU thin-film coating design + reverse engineering

- **Wound:** buyer-comparable lens refuted (confidence 3). Moat-incumbents-legal lens: not refuted.
- **What:** Pip-installable, GPU multilayer optical coating design (needle/global optimisation, tolerance and yield Monte Carlo) plus reverse engineering of deposited stacks from spectrophotometer/ellipsometer data with calibrated uncertainty; cross-platform. Field: optical thin films.
- **Buyer and price:** Coating engineers at optical coating shops, laser-mirror/filter makers, deposition groups. Proposed $99-399/mo. **Wound detail:** the "$2-6k per seat" the finalist claimed they pay today could not be verified anywhere — no incumbent publishes a price. Market sizing was inflated: OptiLayer's own page says "many dozens of users in all corners of the world" (https://optilayer.com/about-us/), not thousands; the "700+ organisations" claim for FilmStar does not appear on https://www.ftgsoftware.com/. Free baseline `tmm` has 266 stars, ~12.6k PyPI downloads/month (https://pypistats.org/packages/tmm).
- **Why big companies avoid it:** A few thousand seats worldwide at most; Zemax carries only a coating module, Lumerical does not target coatings; every incumbent is 1-3 people. Confirmed by the moat lens: no big vendor has a competing full coating suite.
- **Verified comparable:** **NONE FOUND** with sourced revenue. OTF Studio GmbH was registered only 13 Jan 2023 (Amtsgericht München HRB 281582, EUR 25k capital — https://www.northdata.com/OTF+Studio+GmbH,+Garching/HRB+281582), founders Trubetskov and Amochkina (https://www.otfstudio.com/about-us), sold via six regional distributors (https://www.otfstudio.com/imprint). OptiLayer GmbH (Berlin HRB 278717 B) lists a founder, CEO, CTO and signatory, no revenue. Essential Macleod sells through eight named agents (https://www.thinfilmcenter.com/agents.php). TFCalc was sold to Hulinks 17 June 2022, quote-form only (https://www.hulinks.co.jp/software/tfcalc/). FilmStar (https://www.ftgsoftware.com/) has a Clover card link but no listed price. Shape is real; revenue is not.
- **Moat (rewritten per the moat lens):** *not* the differentiable transfer-matrix kernel — that is already MIT open source: tmm_fast (OSRAM, PyTorch, GPU, autograd, v0.3.0 Aug 2026 — https://github.com/MLResearchAtOSRAM/tmm_fast), tmmax (JAX — https://github.com/bahremsd/tmmax), katmer, pyElli (GPL ellipsometry fitting, v0.23.1 Jul 2026 — https://pypi.org/project/pyElli/). Reverse engineering is table stakes: every incumbent ships it (OptiRE, OTF Studio RE, Essential Macleod, FilmStar MEASURE), and ellipsometer vendors bundle fitting free (Woollam CompleteEASE — https://www.jawoollam.com/ellipsometry-software/completeease). The only defensible layer is the optimiser + calibrated inverse-problem engine (global multistart needle, Monte-Carlo yield, regularised UQ on n,k/thickness fits), benchmarked publicly against OIC design-contest results — and Trubetskov won every OIC contest 2007-2019.
- **Kill risk:** The category's observed channel over 30 years is quote/distributor; the paid value incumbents lean on is inline chamber-monitor re-optimisation (OTF Studio taReo — https://www.otfstudio.com/tareo; OptiLayer OptiReOpt), which needs hardware partnerships the owner cannot make.
- **India-legal:** Clean. Wassenaar/EAR 6E003.a controls only production technology for >=500 mm low-loss optics; 6D software entries do not cover stack design; SCOMET mirrors Wassenaar (https://www.dgft.gov.in/CP/?opt=scomet). Paddle/Stripe MoR with GST LUT.
- **30-day public probe:** Ship an open-source PyTorch needle-optimisation layer on top of tmm_fast (adopt, don't rewrite) with two written benchmarks (OIC contest problems; a reverse-engineering example from published ellipsometry data) and a free web calculator. Measure GitHub stars from coating-industry accounts, PyPI downloads, inbound company emails, and clicks on a "Pro: reverse engineering + tolerance/yield, $199/mo" waitlist. Threshold: 30+ company-domain waitlist sign-ups in 30 days. The probe is cheap and would settle the channel question the skeptic could not.
- **Fit:** 5.

### 2.3 WOUNDED — Network-RTK correction engine for small independent CORS networks

- **Wound:** buyer-comparable lens refuted (confidence 3). Moat-incumbents-legal lens: not refuted — this is the one finalist where the OSS gap is genuinely empty.
- **What:** Self-serve VRS/MAC/FKP network-RTK server software (network ambiguity fixing, iono/tropo interpolation, integrity monitoring, RTCM3/NTRIP out), licensed per reference station at $100-300/station/month for 5-50 station regional networks. Field: GNSS infrastructure.
- **Buyer and price:** Independent CORS operators (ag dealers, drone/survey co-ops, construction firms, regional resellers, universities). **Wound detail:** no vendor anywhere sells network-RTK server software by card — Trimble Pivot, Leica Spider, Topcon TopNET and Geo++ GNSMART are all quote-only (https://www.geopp.de/gnsmart-network-rtk/ publishes no price or licensing model; the "per receiver" claim is unverified). The closest self-serve buyers pay SNIP $1,050-$4,850 **once, lifetime** (https://www.use-snip.com/quick-pricing/); the finalist asks a 10-station network for ~$29k/yr recurring. The buyer pool is also shrinking: rover-side aggregators sell corrections direct — Point One True RTK $125/mo (https://pointonenav.com/pricing/), RTKdata $40/mo, 20,000+ stations, "10,000+ customers" (https://rtkdata.com/pricing), onocoy 7,900+ stations pay-per-second (https://www.onocoy.com/), GEODNET targeting 100k stations (https://docs.geodnet.com/). rtklibexplorer, the cited channel, has no network-RTK posts.
- **Why big companies avoid it:** Trimble Pivot's RTXNet is sized for "500 or more stations" and sold via distributors (https://geospatial.trimble.com/en/products/software/trimble-pivot-platform); a 10-station ag dealer is below their sales cost. **Corrected:** Chinese receiver vendors bundle network-RTK server software with reference stations (ComNav CDC.Net — https://www.comnavtech.com/index/detail/47.html; CHCNAV/Hi-Target/South analogous, pages unreachable) — hardware-tied and reseller-sold, a pricing-pressure risk rather than a disqualifier.
- **Verified comparable:** **NONE FOUND.** SNIP / SubCarrier Systems (https://www.use-snip.com/about-snip/) self-describes as "a small consultancy", discloses no headcount or revenue, and is a caster, not a network-RTK engine; its NEARest feature is routing-only, explicitly "as opposed to creating a unique stream ... such as a ... Virtual Reference Station" (https://www.use-snip.com/kb/knowledge-base/nearest-mountpt-use/). The "thousands of caster operators" claim is unverified.
- **Moat:** Real and unfilled. No open-source network-RTK engine exists: RTKBase (766 stars) is single-base (https://github.com/Stefal/rtkbase); Ginan (342 stars) does PPP/SSR products, not VRS (https://github.com/GeoscienceAustralia/ginan); Emlid Caster is "not a network RTK service" (https://emlid.com/ntrip-caster/); Centipede is single-base RTCM3 (https://docs.centipede.fr/). Network ambiguity fixing is not a quarter's work; SNIP has not added it in ~10 years.
- **Kill risk:** PPP-RTK/SSR erosion (Galileo HAS free, PointPerfect, RTX) plus the aggregator marketplaces above remove the reason to run a small CORS network at all; fix reliability is safety-relevant for machine control, so buyers may insist on Trimble/Leica names and a wrong fix is a liability the owner carries.
- **India-legal:** Legal. 2021 Geospatial Guidelines permit Indian entities to operate CORS (guideline PDF 404'd, not re-verified); Survey of India runs a public CORS network (https://www.surveyofindia.gov.in/pages/continuously-operating-reference-stations-cors-); civil RTCM/NTRIP software is not export-controlled.
- **30-day public probe:** Release an open-source single-baseline NTRIP relay with an RTCM3 integrity dashboard on GitHub, plus a blog series showing VRS generated from 3 public EUREF/NOAA stations with fix-rate vs baseline-length plots against single-base. Post to rtklibexplorer, RTK2GO/SNIP forum, GPS World comments, ag-tech forums. Landing page with per-station pricing and a "run this on my network" form. Success = 20+ operator sign-ups with station counts, at least 5 with >5 stations. **Added gate:** at least 3 sign-ups must state they are *not* already reselling an aggregator's corrections.
- **Fit:** 5 (Kalman, optimisation, low-level systems apply directly).

### 2.4 WOUNDED — KinFit: rigorous global kinetics and binding-analysis engine

- **Wound:** buyer-comparable lens refuted (confidence 3) on Filter 7 (NONE FOUND) and a price ceiling that falls below the brief's floor. Moat-incumbents-legal lens: not refuted, with the moat claim corrected.
- **What:** Vendor-neutral fitting engine for Biacore/Octet/ITC/stopped-flow exports returning globally fitted rate constants with profile-likelihood/Bayesian CIs, identifiability diagnostics, model discrimination and reproducible batch reports. Field: biophysical interaction analysis.
- **Buyer and price:** Biophysics/assay scientists at small biotechs, CROs, antibody shops, academic cores whose deliverable is the KD. Proposed $600/yr academic, $2,400/yr commercial, $8k site. **Wound detail:** the only verified self-serve card prices in the niche are AFFINImeter "from EUR 300 / from EUR 500" per year (https://www.affinimeter.com/site/software/) — and AFFINImeter is ITC/NMR only, not SPR/BLI, and also sells consulting; Scrubber2 $490/yr academic (site currently serves an expired TLS certificate); KinTek sells by card but hides prices behind a licence selector (https://www.kintekexplorer.com/purchase/). The $2,400 commercial seat and $8k site licences that carry the revenue arithmetic are PO-driven biotech/CRO procurement — Scrubber2's own "site licences by PO" — i.e. the motion the owner has ruled out. At EUR 300-500/yr (~$25-40/mo) the verified tier is hobbyist-priced under Filter 3.
- **Why big companies avoid it:** Instrument vendors bundle "good enough" evaluation software to sell hardware and have no incentive to read competitors' files or expose bad published KDs; third-party licence market ~$5-20M/yr. Genedata Screener already sells an SPR/BLI batch module enterprise-only (https://www.sprpages.nl/spr-software).
- **Verified comparable:** **NONE FOUND** — the finalist itself wrote "Revenue undisclosed for all; longevity is the evidence." BioKin/DynaFit (http://www.biokin.com/dynafit/ — connection reset), KinTek Corp (https://www.kintekcorp.com/about/, 30+ years, no headcount/revenue), BioLogic Scrubber2, AFFINImeter.
- **Moat (corrected):** "Identifiability diagnostics and model comparison no existing tool does" is false — KinTek Explorer's FitSpace confidence contours and DynaFit's Monte-Carlo CIs + AIC discrimination have done this for ~25 years. What remains: vendor-neutral bit-exact SPR/BLI parsers + cheap differentiable-sensitivity CIs + deterministic batch reports. Free competition is larger than listed: Anabel (CRAN v3.0.2, hosted at anabel-online.com — https://github.com/SKscience/Anabel), pyKinGenie (MIT, parses Octet .frd and Gator, hosted free as KinGenie on EMBL Hamburg's EU-funded eSPC — https://github.com/osvalB/pykingenie, https://spc.embl-hamburg.de/), htrSPRanalysis (CRAN, global fit, multi-core batch, PDF reports — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13446736/). The GitHub SPR topic has 6 repos, max 3 stars: little free pressure, but also little evidence of a practitioner community that would find a PyPI package.
- **Kill risk:** Most users need a 1:1 Langmuir KD, not a credible interval; willingness to pay collapses to the academic tier and $1M needs ~2,000 seats.
- **India-legal:** Clean. Pure software on user-exported CSV; no regulated data; design avoids reverse-engineering binary vendor run files (EULA risk).
- **30-day public probe:** Ship `kinfit` on PyPI (MIT core; CIs/batch behind a licence key) with Biacore and Octet parsers; publish a benchmark of fitted KDs and CI coverage on public sensorgram datasets vs Anabel, KinetiHub, pyKinGenie and vendor software. Post to SPRpages, Biostars, ResearchGate, Biacore/Octet LinkedIn groups. Success in 30 days: >300 non-CI installs, >=5 unsolicited "can we pay for site licence/batch" emails, >=3 card pre-orders at $2,400/yr. Zero pre-orders = kill.
- **Fit:** 5.

## 3. Killed by skeptics

- **Stochastic FELA (geotech, per-run GPU limit analysis)** — both lenses, confidence 4: Rocscience Slide2 has shipped random-field spatial variability with Pf output since ~2017 inside the $2,599/yr Personal lease bought by card (https://www.rocscience.com/help/slide2/documentation/slide-model/project-settings/statistics/spatial-variability, https://www.rocscience.com/plans-pricing/price-list); OptumCE published "Reliability Analysis in OPTUM GX" on 4 Aug 2026 and its "Buy" buttons route to a quote form (https://optumce.com/pricing-commercial/); rigorous FELA bounds for bearing capacity are free for commercial use (https://github.com/geoeq/GeoEQ-Cap). Only "GPU is faster" remained, and that is a quarter's feature for incumbents who own the mesh, solver and post-processing.
- **FastRietveld (GPU batch powder-diffraction refinement)** — moat lens, confidence 3: TOPAS-Academic V8 already headlines "Parametric, in situ and operando studies across thousands of data sets" (https://topas-academic.com/) — the comparable *is* the incumbent occupying the exact kernel-licensed-to-Bruker slot; GSAS-II ships sequential refinement, parametric fits vs T/time with ESD propagation and analytic Jacobians (https://github.com/AdvancedPhotonSource/GSAS-II); per-pattern refinements are embarrassingly parallel, so GPU throughput is not a durable moat.
- **CellFit (cycler data to calibrated cell model with UQ)** — moat lens, confidence 3: every moat line item is free from the field's own authors — PyBOP v26.3 ships MCMC samplers, Hessian/SALib sensitivity, HPPC multi-pulse identification and an "identification pitfalls" notebook (https://github.com/pybop-team/PyBOP); Ionworks (5 people, YC S24, PyBaMM creators) open-sourced its cycler parser layer as `ionworksdata` (MIT — https://github.com/ionworks/ionworksdata) and sells the exact product demo-led; PyProBE has ICA/DVA/DMA and the "phantom LAM/LLI" paper is from its own group. This is the killed shape "platform vendor gives the engine away".
- **TrackerFree (laser-tracker-free robot calibration + dynamics ID)** — both lenses, confidence 4: the finalist's stated biggest kill risk had already happened — the cited Nature Comms Eng paper (MUKCa) ships MIT code and the printable fixture STL with an ABB Robotics co-author (https://github.com/platonics-delft/kinematics_calibration, 120 stars); the "Pro" tier is free in FIGAROH-Plus (Apache-2.0, on PyPI: planar-contact calibration, IPOPT excitation design, SDP/LMI-consistent dynamics ID — https://github.com/thanhndv212/figaroh-plus) and FloBaRoID; RoboDK is a distributor-sold company with sales roles and a EUR 17,995 calibration licence (https://robodk.com/about, https://robodk.com/pricing); the most-starred repo in the category ever has 221 stars, below the probe's own 300-star bar.
- **Sheetbin (true-shape nesting as API + web app)** — both lenses, confidence 3-4: sparrow + jagua-rs (MIT/MPL, SIMD Rust) set new best-known solutions on all 13 ESICUP instances (https://arxiv.org/html/2509.13329) and ship the proposed probe artefact for free at https://sparrowstudio.app/ — the benchmark position the moat depends on is already taken and freely embeddable; Efficient Software Ltd reports 8 employees, withheld its P&L, and NestFab's only CTA is "contact us for a formal quotation" (https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/08918202, https://www.nestfab.com/pricing); no self-serve nesting API with published pricing exists anywhere; the verified card tier is $25-49/mo marketed to hobbyists.
- **StripFix (UAV LiDAR strip adjustment + boresight self-calibration)** — moat lens, confidence 4: DJI Terra's L2 Point Cloud Accuracy Optimization is completely free (https://enterprise.dji.com/dji-terra/faq); YellowScan CloudStation ships robust and time-dependent strip adjustment with accuracy/precision/mismatch reports (https://www.yellowscan.com/products/cloudstation/); Phoenix SpatialPro ships LiDARSnap; TU Wien OPALS StripAdjust implements the full rigorous model (https://opals.geo.tuwien.ac.at/html/stable/ModuleStripAdjust.html); BayesMap already markets "Uncertainty maps - a unique feature" (https://bayesmap.com/), removing the claimed differentiator. Finalist conceded Filter 7 NONE FOUND. (Price correction: TerraMatch UAV is EUR 1,620/yr, not 972 — https://terrasolid.com/pricing/.)

## 6. Cross-cutting pattern

**What the four survivors share.** All four are *inverse-problem + certification* engines: take a practitioner's measured data (spectra, S-parameters, sensorgrams, GNSS observables), fit a physical model, and return a result *with a calibrated statement of how much to trust it* (uncertainty, passivity certificate, identifiability verdict, fix-rate curve). All four sit in niches where the incumbents are 1-3 person shops running 1990s-era Windows code, where the differentiable/GPU *kernel* is already open source but the rigour layer on top is not, and where nobody — incumbent or comparable — publishes revenue. All four have a 30-day probe that needs no hardware. That is the owner's genuine sweet spot, and it is a real pattern: the "rigour layer over a free kernel" is where numerical skill still commands a price. The problem is not the engineering thesis; it is that none of these niches has produced a demonstrated no-selling $500k business.

**Which filter killed the most.** Ranked by casualties:

1. **Filter 7 (named comparable with sourced revenue)** — 10 of 10 finalists and 17 of 17 dropped. Zero candidates in the entire scan cleared it. Every comparable was either revenue-undisclosed (sole traders and GbRs file nothing), turned out to be 8-25 people with sales/distributors on verification, or was a price-point proof only. This filter is now doing the work of a null result: it did not discriminate between candidates because nothing passed it.
2. **Filter 1/4 jointly (moat already shipped free or by the incumbent)** — 6 of 10 finalists (FELA, Rietveld, CellFit, TrackerFree, Sheetbin, StripFix) and roughly half the empty sweeps. The recurring mechanism: an academic group or the incumbent's own founder open-sourced the exact wedge in 2024-2026, often with an OEM co-author (ABB on MUKCa, OSRAM on tmm_fast, Ionworks on ionworksdata, Merck on BayBE). "Platform vendor gives it away" has generalised from NVIDIA/Meta to *any* well-funded lab or 5-person YC company.
3. **Filter 2 (no selling — buyers pay by card)** — 3 of 4 survivors carry this wound (coatings, network RTK, kinetics), and it underlies many empties. The finding is not that self-serve is impossible; it is that in conservative engineering niches the *observed* channel over decades is quote and distributor, and the card tier that exists is priced for individuals.
4. **Filter 5 (India-legal)** — killed only two sub-shapes (GNSS jamming simulators, launch trajectory optimisation). Not a binding constraint.
5. **Filter 6 (value on day one)** — bound only where validation needs physical hardware (FDTD antennas, MotorMap, Warpcheck, EmberGPU). The four survivors all avoid this.

**What this says about where to look next, or whether to stop.** Three scans and ~100 agents have now produced the same shape of result: strong engineering theses, zero verified comparables. Two readings are possible, and the owner has to pick one before spending a fourth scan:

- *Reading A — the bar is miscalibrated.* Filter 7 as written ("<=5 people, no sales team, sourced $500k-$1M") may describe a business that exists but never publishes — sole traders and GbRs in Germany, Australia and the UK do not file revenue, and the scans keep finding 20-30-year-old solo vendors (Coelho, FTG, BioKin, SubCarrier, AtaiTec, rapidlasso) whose longevity *is* the only evidence available. If the owner accepts "longevity + published prices + no visible sales function" as a proxy for sourced revenue, then the PCB SI toolkit passes cleanly and CoatFit passes on the moat lens. The rational next step under Reading A is **not another scan** but running the PCB SI 30-day probe (cheapest, no hardware, both skeptic lenses passed) and, in parallel, the CoatFit probe, and letting real sign-up counts replace the missing revenue figure.

- *Reading B — the bar is right and the niches fail it.* If a sourced revenue figure is non-negotiable, the evidence from three scans is that engineering-simulation verticals do not contain the target business: the buyers are conservative, the channel is quote, the small survivors are quote-sellers, and the card tier is hobbyist-priced. Under Reading B, further scanning of simulation/analysis verticals is expected to return more of the same, and the honest recommendation is to **stop scanning this shape**. The only unexplored directions the scans hint at are (i) shapes where buyers are *developers* rather than domain engineers, since developers demonstrably pay by card — but devtools was killed in scan 1 on the "platform gives it away" pattern; and (ii) selling the *certificate* rather than the engine (a validated-model report that a customer attaches to a deliverable), which is closer to a service than a product and sits uneasily with Filter 1.

Recommendation: do not commission a fourth field scan. Decide between Reading A and B. If A, run the PCB SI probe with the skeptic's added gate (must beat scikit-rf and MMTL on a public validation set) and the CoatFit probe (build on tmm_fast, benchmark against OIC contest results); kill both at 30 days on the stated thresholds. If B, close the venture-scan workstream and record that Filter 7 was the binding constraint across all three scans.
