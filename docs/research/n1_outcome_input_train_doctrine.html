Doctrine: N1 Outcome vs Input (Train Architecture)

Date: 2026-06-04  |  Status: Canonical train doctrine  |  Owner: This file (all others link here)

One line: Commodity futures return is the outcome (the exam). Raw linked intelligence is the input (the moat). Commoditized controls are proof-of-concept hygiene — not the product.

Aligns with [[agent_playground_doctrine]] (edge in construction + linkage, not bare series) and [[full_quant_base_model]] (base ℰ → niche readout). Approved founder direction 2026-06-04.

1. Three roles (do not conflate)

2. What is not the edge

  • Past-price momentum block (PRICE_FEAT 9 dims on instrument nodes) — table stakes; minimize as input for N1 edge runs.
  • Options IV — deferred; masked via TIRRA_MASK_M15_OPTIONS=1 until paid/historical chains exist.
  • DeFi TVL — high row count, low N1 edge; cap or exclude from commodity POC prefetch (N3 playground).
  • GDELT at 100% — noise flood (V46 lesson); cap at train time, do not eliminate entirely (N4 geo input).

instrument_daily remains in the pipeline for label construction, backtests, and gate freshness — not as the primary intelligence story on instrument nodes.

DB row counts ≠ train exposure. DeFi + GDELT can dominate SQL; subsampling and entity-type graph structure determine what biases ℰ. Verify training log: GDELT subsample frac=… before trusting a run.
