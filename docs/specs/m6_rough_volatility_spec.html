1. Design Objectives

The module agent/quant/rough.py will provide high-performance, vectorized, and fully differentiable rough volatility simulation and estimation tools in PyTorch. It must support:

  • Differentiable generation of fractional Volterra paths using the Bennedsen-Lunde-Pakkanen (BLP) hybrid scheme.
  • rBergomi variance path simulation and correlated stock price paths.
  • Empirical Hurst exponent estimation from raw timeseries.

3. Numerical Stability & Limit Guards

  • Hurst Index Clamp: $H \in (0, 0.5)$ must be strictly clamped to $[0.01, 0.499]$ inside calculations to prevent NaN division in the singular kernel weight calculation (as $H \rightarrow 0.5$, weight denominators contain $H - 0.5 \rightarrow 0$).
  • Vol-of-Vol Clamp: $\eta \ge 1e-4$.
  • Correlation Clamp: $\rho \in [-0.999, 0.999]$.

4. Test Criteria

  • Verify estimate_hurst_exponent correctly extracts Hurst $H < 0.5$ on generated fractional paths.
  • Verify RoughBergomiModel generates positive variance paths.
  • Verify ATM implied skew under rBergomi scales roughly as $T^{H-1/2}$ for short maturities (by pricing simulated paths at various maturities and fitting the log-skew vs log-maturity slope).
